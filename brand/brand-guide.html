Brand Pack

Skoga

The visual identity for Skoga and its mascot, Kongle — a little evergreen who keeps a calm eye on your team of agents. (Kongle is Norwegian for “pinecone”; he is, technically, a conifer. We like the joke.)

Logo

The lockup

Stacked — square spaces
Reversed — dark surfaces
Wordmark only
  • The wordmark is set in Newsreader (weight 500) and is shipped as outlined vector paths — it does not depend on the font loading and will never reflow.
  • Keep clear space around the lockup equal to the height of Kongle’s sprout. Don’t recolor, rotate, stretch, or add effects to the mark.
