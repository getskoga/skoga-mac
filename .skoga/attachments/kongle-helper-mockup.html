Kongle Helper

A concept mockup of Kongle as a friendly corner helper for Skoga — file a bug, request a feature, or ask for help. He emotes through the flow and potters about while you work.

Click Kongle to open him. Close him (the ×) and leave him be to watch him idle — reading, headphones, coffee, scribbling, dozing.
